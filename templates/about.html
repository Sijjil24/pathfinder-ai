About

How PathFinder AI works

A career recommendation tool made for interns, built entirely with free tools.

1. TensorFlow matches roles

A small neural network, trained on a skills-to-roles dataset, scores how well your profile fits each job role.

2. AI writes your path

Llama models on Groq's free tier turn your skill gaps into a step-by-step plan with free resources.

3. SQLite saves everything

Your profiles, recommendations and completed steps are stored so you can come back any time.

4. Secure sign-in

Passwords are hashed. Google sign-in and reset emails run through Firebase Authentication.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Flask, TensorFlow, SQLite, the Groq free tier and Firebase's free plan cost nothing.

What if I have no Groq key?

The app still works. It uses a built-in learning path template until you add a key.

Can I add more job roles?

Yes. Edit data/roles_skills.csv, then run the training script again.
